    Self-evaluation:
      Why Practice Matters

      Train like it's the real thing.

      Speaking fluently on complex international topics — under time pressure, without preparation — is one of the most demanding skills in any diplomatic service exam. It is not just about what you know, but how confidently and clearly you can structure your thoughts on the spot.

      ✦
      Build real fluency

      Regular repetition trains your mind to find structure and language quickly — even on topics you haven't studied in depth.

      ⏱
      Simulate exam conditions

      Random topics, real timers, and structured reflection mirror exactly what you'll face — so exam day feels familiar, not frightening.

      ◈
      Grow with every round

      Your notes and self-evaluation after each session turn practice into progress — helping you identify patterns and sharpen your delivery over time.
